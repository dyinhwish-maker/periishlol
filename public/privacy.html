Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information periish.lol collects, how we use it, how long we keep it, and what control you have over your data.

We collect information needed to provide, secure, and improve periish.lol. This may include account information, profile content you choose to provide, uploaded content, usage information, security signals, and purchase or billing information.

Some information is provided directly by you, such as when you create an account, customize your profile, upload content, contact support, or make a purchase. Other information is collected automatically to keep the service secure, measure basic usage, and prevent abuse. This may include country-level location, hashed IP addresses, session data, and limited browser or device signals.

We use information to operate periish.lol, manage accounts, display and customize profiles, store uploaded content, process purchases, provide support, recover accounts, improve the service, protect users, prevent abuse, and enforce our Terms of Service.

We process your information only when we have a reason to do so. Most of the time, this is because we need the information to provide periish.lol, keep accounts secure, process purchases, prevent abuse, provide support, or comply with legal obligations.

Where required by privacy laws, our legal reasons may include providing the service you requested, protecting periish.lol and its users, meeting legal or payment obligations, or using your consent for optional features.

periish.lol uses cookies and similar technologies to keep users signed in, maintain secure sessions, remember service-related preferences, measure basic usage, and protect against abuse.
